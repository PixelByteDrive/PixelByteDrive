PixelByteDrive - A bio in 1.2gb/s speeds.

At a lunch table in a mid-kinda school, PixelByteDrive was launched as a test subject for exploring many elements in HTML. Little did he (the author of this article) know, it was about to be a dedicated site for everything that he ever done. With multiple groups in many platforms, he decided to unite them all to make PixelByteDrive the parent of these subsidiaries. But there was more than meets the eye...

Months ago, he had tested many of his skills on a mini-projected identified as NewsPager, containing of very simple elements compiled as a news service but as a privately owned project that only he knew about. Killian was very optimistic about this program and thought it may gain a fair amount of popularity, but hasn't recognized the complexity of maintaining a site with 100% integrity and eventually abandoned the project.

Advancing into March, Andrew Chen (his co-operative partner) had helped to the best of his immense knowledge, as well as his expandable resources by providing many services that helped the expansion of the project.

Killian - The keyboard is on fire, and it's his fault.

The concept that Killian had in mind for a community-like project had been traced far back when Studio Club, and other groups were launched; their intentions were to allow everyone to create the Internet. While it's actual intention was to bring his friends together, it was dedicated to shaping what the future would look like. Killian has taken partial dedication of his days creating new experience that even he has never seen before using the only tools that the services provide, and it shows.

There was no stopping now. He loved what he did and had an idea that it could become something cool, only knowing that he has to do as much as he could to maybe discover what a community wants best.

Dedication. Making the most of what we have while we have it.

PixelByteDrive was originally founded to test the web to its maximum advantage. Today, it is the holder of multiple subsidiaries that create the best of what programs are capable of doing to it's potential.

We want to create the web that can do what no other site has done. We want to use what we can to the maximum extent possible, pushing the limits of what seemed impossible now possible, and that was by trying.
